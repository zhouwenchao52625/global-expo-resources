Overseas exhibition logistics checklist

A freight quotation is only one part of the exhibition plan. The venue, customs route and return handling can each create a separate deadline.

Confirm before booking

  • Who handles export documents, customs clearance and delivery to the stand?
  • What are the venue access windows, lifting rules and advance registration deadlines?
  • Which materials will return, be disposed of or remain in the destination market?
  • Are storage, demurrage and late handling charges excluded from the quotation?
