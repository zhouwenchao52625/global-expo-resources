International trade fair selection checklist

Shortlisting a show should start with the target buyer and a verifiable edition, not with the largest advertised attendance number.

Before booking space

  • Confirm that the organizer, venue, city and edition year match the listing.
  • Check the confirmed dates separately from projected dates or old schedules.
  • Compare the product scope and buyer profile with the actual sales objective.
  • Review the evidence behind visitor and exhibitor claims, including its year and method.
  • Build the complete participation budget and operational timeline.
