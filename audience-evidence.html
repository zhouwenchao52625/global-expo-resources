Reviewing exhibition audience evidence

A large attendance number is not enough to prove that a show reaches the buyers relevant to a product or market. Look for the source, date and definition behind the claim.

What to verify

  • Whether visitor and exhibitor figures refer to the latest confirmed edition.
  • Whether the organizer defines unique visitors, registrations and repeat entries consistently.
  • Whether the published buyer profile matches the target industry and decision role.
  • Whether claimed exhibitors are current, relevant and distributed across the intended market.

Keep organizer statements, third-party data and internal assumptions separate. Document what is confirmed before using the evidence in a budget request.
