Post-show follow-up plan

A badge scan becomes useful only when the conversation, buyer need and agreed next step are recorded before the team leaves the exhibition.

Build the handoff

  • Assign an owner for lead cleaning and first follow-up before the show opens.
  • Record product interest, current supplier, timing and decision role at the booth.
  • Sort by documented need rather than the number of scans collected.
  • Send a useful recap with one clear next step while the conversation is still fresh.
  • Schedule a second touch for buyers who need technical information or a sample.
