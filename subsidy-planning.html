Overseas exhibition subsidy planning

An exhibition subsidy should be checked against primary documents before it is included in a booth decision. The useful evidence is not a social-media summary, but the notice, the applicable catalogue, and the current application window.

Four checks

  1. Confirm the policy year and the dates covered by the exhibition activity.
  2. Match the applicant jurisdiction with the authority issuing the notice.
  3. Check that the exact event appears in the relevant exhibition catalogue.
  4. Record the documents required for the eligible costs and retain the source trail.
