An audit-ready exhibition budget

An overseas exhibition budget needs a source for every material estimate, not just one approved total. Keep organizer charges, services, travel, freight and contingency visible by line.

Control points

  • Record the quotation currency, exchange-rate date and payment schedule.
  • Attach a quotation, venue tariff or policy notice to every estimate.
  • Separate eligible subsidy costs from the full project budget.
  • Preserve the approved version when a date, hall or service package changes.
  • Reconcile final invoices with the approved estimate and explain material variances.
