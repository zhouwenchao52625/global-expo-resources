International trade fair verification checklist

A short listing can look convincing while carrying outdated dates, changed venues, or the wrong buyer profile. Use these checks before committing a booth budget.

1. Confirm the organizer and source page

Compare the event name with the organizer's own domain. A reliable organizer page should connect the event, venue, current dates, and exhibitor application process in one place.

2. Separate confirmed dates from projections

If the next edition has not been announced, label the date as projected. Do not treat an estimate copied from an old listing as a confirmed event schedule.

3. Verify the venue and city

Exhibition centers change names and organizers sometimes move halls or cities. Check the current venue against the latest organizer announcement before booking travel or freight.

4. Review exhibitor and visitor evidence

Look for the buyer profile, product categories, exhibitor count, and visitor data published by the organizer. When those figures are not available, record “official data not published” instead of estimating.

5. Check the full participation cost

The booth fee is only one line. Confirm electricity, furniture, registration, deposits, freight, customs, travel, accommodation, marketing, and post-show follow-up before approving the budget.

6. Record the verification date

Save the source URL and the date on which you checked it. This makes the decision auditable and tells colleagues whether the information is still current.
